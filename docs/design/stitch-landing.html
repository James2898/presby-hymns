Frequently Asked Questions

Presbyterian Hymns is maintained for churches, choirs, youth fellowships, and presbyteries.

Why is this distributed as an APK rather than the Play Store?

Hosting directly via GitHub Releases allows our community to deliver free, immediate updates without commercial platform developer fees, arbitrary policy removals, or mandatory Google Account logins.

Are the Tagalog hymns matched verse-for-verse?

Yes. Where standard historical Tagalog versions exist (such as from the classical Evangelical Presbyterian Himnaryo traditions), stanzas and refrains follow corresponding liturgical meters.

Can church guitarists customize chord voicings?

Yes. The app incorporates interactive transpose buttons (-1 / +1), Capo selectors from fret 0 through 7, and a one-touch toggle to hide chords completely for reading congregants.

How can I submit a typo or suggest a hymn?

Our repository is completely public on GitHub. Anyone can open an Issue to request lyrical revisions, submit chord corrections, or contribute pull requests for unlisted Tagalog canticles.
