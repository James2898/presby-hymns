- **Tier:** 1
- **Date Created:** 2026-11-29

## Feature Summary

Redesign the Home screen navigation to consolidate English and Tagalog hymn entry points into a single unified "Hymns" `CatalogCard` (opening the Hymn Index on the `ALL` filter with combined hymn count), add a Tagalog Responsive Reading navigation card (`"Tugunang Pagbasa"`) leading to a searchable numbered Readings List and dedicated Reading Detail screen, and add a disabled `"Westminster Shorter Catechism"` (`"Coming soon"`) placeholder card, while removing the Home AppBar "Hymn Index" icon. On the content side, parse all 57 Tagalog responsive readings from `raw_content/Tagalog Responsive Readings.md` via `tool/build_content.dart` into `assets/db/hymns.db` with a `language` column for future English support and bump `kAppDbVersion` for cache synchronization.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Home Screen Body Structure:** The Home screen body displays strictly these sections from top to bottom: (1) Hero banner, (2) Search bar (with hymn search results dropdown when query is non-empty), (3) `"Hymns"` `CatalogCard`, (4) `"Tugunang Pagbasa"` `CatalogCard`, and (5) `"Westminster Shorter Catechism"` `CatalogCard`. Separate English and Tagalog hymn cards are no longer displayed on the Home screen.
2. **Home AppBar:** The Home screen AppBar retains the title `"Presbyterian Hymns"` and removes the `"Hymn Index"` icon button (preserving any unrelated existing AppBar elements — `<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`).
3. **Home Search Scope:** Home screen search remains hymns-only; entering text matching responsive reading titles, numbers, or body lines returns no responsive reading results in the home search dropdown.
4. **CatalogCard Extensions:** `CatalogCard` supports (a) optional/nullable count such that omitting the count renders no count pill/text and leaves no broken empty spacing, and (b) a disabled state supporting an optional badge (e.g., `"Coming soon"`), non-interactive card and button callbacks, disabled visual styling (`<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`), and disabled accessibility semantics. All existing `CatalogCard` callers remain compatible.
5. **Unified Hymns Card:** The `"Hymns"` `CatalogCard` displays the combined total count of all hymns (English and Tagalog combined) from the database and, when tapped (card or action button), navigates to the Hymn Index screen opened on the `ALL` filter (`<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`). *(Amended by **AM-1** below: use unique hymn count `COUNT(*) FROM hymns` = 173, matching `IndexTab.all`.)*
6. **Responsive Reading Home Card:** The Responsive Reading `CatalogCard` displays title `"Tugunang Pagbasa"`, button label `"Tingnan ang mga Pagbasa"`, and no count; tapping the card or button navigates to the Readings List screen.
7. **Westminster Shorter Catechism Card:** The `"Westminster Shorter Catechism"` `CatalogCard` displays a `"Coming soon"` badge and no count, renders in a disabled state (including semantic disabled state for assistive technologies), and performs no navigation or action when the card or its button is tapped.
8. **Database Schema & Language Support:** Drift schema and `assets/db/hymns.db` store responsive readings with `language` (populated with the Tagalog code `<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`, e.g., `TL`), `number`, `title`, and `scripture_reference` (or `<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`), enforcing uniqueness on `(language, number)`, plus ordered body line entries (`line_order`, `role` in `{'leader', 'people', 'all'}`, and `text`).
9. **Build-Time Markdown Ingestion:** `tool/build_content.dart` parses `raw_content/Tagalog Responsive Readings.md` into `assets/db/hymns.db`, producing all 57 consecutive readings numbered `1` through `57`.
10. **Line-by-Line Splitting & Normalization:** The parser normalizes `\r\n` to `\n` and processes lines individually (not splitting on `\n\n`, ensuring readings such as #36 and #37 split accurately despite single-newline `  \n` breaks and no blank separator line), trimming leading/trailing spaces, `\t`, and Unicode em-space (`\u2003`), skipping empty lines, collapsing internal runs of tabs/multiple spaces to a single space, and unescaping `\!` to `!`.
11. **Header Parsing:** Reading headers match `^\*\*\s*T[au]guna?ng?\s+Pagbasa\s+Bilang\s+(\d+):?\s*\*\*$` (case-insensitive, flexible whitespace), extracting integer `number` across all source variations (standard, trailing colon in #8/#11–28, `"Tagunang"` in #9–10, `"Tugunag"` in #55, and double space in #45).
12. **Title Parsing:** The first non-empty line following each header is parsed as `title` by stripping whitespace/tabs (e.g., #43) and outer `**` delimiters while preserving inner text verbatim (including trailing punctuation such as #8).
13. **Body Line Role Parsing:** Lines between the title and trailing reference lines are classified in order:
    - Lines matching `^\*\*\*(.+?)\*\*\*$` -> role `all`, stripping outer `***` (optional per reading; #16, #44, and #50 have zero `all` lines and emit none).
    - Lines matching `^\*\*(.+?)\*\*([.,;:?!]*)$` -> role `leader`, stripping outer `**` and preserving trailing outside punctuation (e.g., periods on #22 and #33).
    - Remaining plain-text body lines -> role `people`.
    Consecutive same-role lines (e.g., #9 consecutive `people` lines, #50 consecutive `leader` lines) are preserved as distinct ordered line records.
14. **Scripture Reference Parsing:** Trailing plain-text line(s) following the final formatted (`**...**` or `***...***`) body line of each reading are collected as `scripture_reference`, joining consecutive multi-line trailing references (e.g., #41) with a single space and preserving source citations verbatim (e.g., `Awit 23`, `Awit 37:513`, `Kaw. 10:1-11`).
15. **Content Validation & Verification:** `tool/build_content.dart` validates all parsed readings (failing with a non-zero exit code if count is 0 or not 57, any reading has an empty title, empty reference, or 0 body lines, duplicate `(language, number)` exists, or residual unstripped `**` markers remain) and matches the sample fixture for Reading #1 (`number = 1`, `title = "Ang Daan Ng Buhay"`, `scripture_reference = "Awit 1:1-6"`, 12 body lines: 6 `leader`, 5 `people`, 1 `all`).
16. **DB Asset Cache Synchronization & Isolation:** `kAppDbVersion` in `lib/data/database/app_database.dart` (and Drift `schemaVersion` if applicable — `<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`) is incremented in the same commit as the updated `assets/db/hymns.db` so existing installs replace stale cached SQLite files without eagerly loading the asset on matching versions (per `MEMORY.md` BUG-004 & BUG-007). All feature code reads via canonical `appDatabaseProvider` and all unit/widget tests override `appDatabaseProvider` with `AppDatabase(NativeDatabase.memory())` (per `MEMORY.md` BUG-002 & BUG-003). *(Amended by **AM-2** below: `kAppDbVersion` lives in `lib/data/database/connection/connection_native.dart`.)*
17. **Readings List Screen:** Displays `"Tugunang Pagbasa"` in the AppBar with back navigation to Home, listing all Tagalog responsive readings sorted ascending by `number` (1–57), with each item showing reading `number`, `title`, and `scripture_reference` (`<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`) without text clipping or overflow.
18. **Readings List Filter & Empty/Error States:** A filter input at the top of the Readings List screen filters readings in real time by matching trimmed `number` or case-insensitive `title` substring, includes a clear action when non-empty, renders a clear empty state message when no readings match (or when DB has 0 readings), and renders appropriate loading/error feedback without crashing.
19. **Readings List Navigation:** Tapping any item on the Readings List screen opens the Reading Detail screen for that reading.
20. **Reading Detail Palette & Header:** The Reading Detail screen uses the Tagalog color palette (`AppColors.canvasTl` background and `accent*` tokens per `<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`), and renders a readable header containing the reading number (`Tugunang Pagbasa Bilang {number}`), `title`, and `scripture_reference`, wrapping multi-line titles and long references (e.g., #41) with zero horizontal overflow.
21. **Reading Detail Body Formatting:** Body lines render sequentially in `line_order` with:
    - `leader`: bold (`FontWeight.bold`), left-aligned.
    - `people`: normal weight (`FontWeight.normal`), right-aligned.
    - `all`: bold and italic (`FontWeight.bold`, `FontStyle.italic`), centered (`TextAlign.center`).
    Readings without an `all` line (#16, #44, #50) render their `leader` and `people` lines cleanly with no blank placeholder row.
22. **Reading Detail Controls (No Music Features):** The Reading Detail screen provides only text-size controls (increase / decrease) and previous / next reading navigation controls. No music controls (chords, Hide Chords, Capo, CAGED, pitch transposition, or audio cue tones) are rendered.
23. **Text-Size Bounds & Persistence:** Increasing or decreasing text size scales reading body copy within bounded min/max limits (`<must match codebase convention — see context.md "Patterns to Follow">`), disables Decrease at minimum size and Increase at maximum size, and retains the active text size across previous/next reading navigation.
24. **Previous / Next Navigation & Scroll Reset:** Previous and Next controls step sequentially by `number` across all Tagalog readings (1–57) and reset vertical scroll position to top on transition; Previous is disabled on Reading #1 and Next is disabled on Reading #57.
25. **Missing Reading Fallback:** Opening the Reading Detail route with a non-existent reading ID/number displays a graceful not-found message with back navigation instead of throwing an unhandled exception.

## Out of Scope

- English Responsive Readings content (DB keeps a `language` column for future support, but only Tagalog readings are parsed and displayed in this task).
- Westminster Shorter Catechism content, list screen, or reader screen (only the disabled `"Coming soon"` Home card is built).
- Including Responsive Readings inside Home screen global search results.
- Musical features on Responsive Readings (chords, Hide Chords, Capo, CAGED, pitch transposition, or cue tones).
- FTS indexing or full-body text search inside Responsive Readings (list filter covers number and title only).
- Separate English/Tagalog hymn cards on the Home screen.
